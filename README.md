# Products Sales Data Analysis Project

## Project Overview

This project focuses on performing **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a retail sales dataset to uncover customer purchasing behavior, sales trends, product performance, and demographic insights. The analysis helps businesses understand their customers better and make data-driven decisions to improve sales and marketing strategies.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
- Identify high-value customer segments.
- Determine top-performing states and regions.
- Discover the most popular product categories.
- Analyze occupation-based purchasing patterns.
- Generate actionable business insights through visualization and data analysis.

---

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains customer transaction records with demographic and purchasing information.

### Features Included

| Category | Attributes |
|-----------|------------|
| Customer Details | Gender, Age Group, Marital Status |
| Geographic Data | State, Zone |
| Occupation Data | Occupation |
| Product Information | Product Category, Product ID |
| Sales Information | Orders, Amount |

## Exploratory Data Analysis

### Gender Analysis

#### Objective
Analyze purchasing behavior based on gender.

#### Insights
- Female customers contribute significantly more to overall sales than male customers.
- Female customers represent the primary customer segment.

#### Business Impact
Marketing campaigns can be targeted toward female customers for higher ROI.

---

### Age Group Analysis

#### Objective
Identify the age groups contributing the most revenue.

#### Insights
- Customers aged **26–35 years** generate the highest sales.
- Young and middle-aged customers dominate purchasing activity.

#### Business Impact
Promotional campaigns should focus on customers within this age bracket.

---

### Marital Status Analysis

#### Objective
Understand how marital status affects purchasing behavior.

#### Insights
- Married customers contribute significantly more sales.
- Married women form one of the most valuable customer segments.

#### Business Impact
Targeted offers for families and married customers can increase revenue.

---

### State-wise Sales Analysis

#### Objective
Identify top-performing states.

#### Insights
Top contributing states include:

- Uttar Pradesh
- Maharashtra
- Karnataka

#### Business Impact
Businesses can allocate more resources and marketing budgets to high-performing regions.

---

### Occupation Analysis

#### Objective
Analyze purchasing behavior across professions.

#### Insights
Top contributing occupations include:

- IT Sector
- Healthcare
- Aviation

#### Business Impact
Occupation-based campaigns can improve customer targeting and engagement.

---

### Product Category Analysis

#### Objective
Determine the most popular product categories.

#### Insights
Highest-performing categories include:

- Food
- Clothing
- Electronics

#### Business Impact
Inventory and marketing efforts should focus on high-demand categories.

---

### Product Performance Analysis

#### Objective
Identify best-selling products.

#### Insights
- Top products generate a significant portion of total revenue.
- Product-level analysis helps optimize inventory planning.

#### Business Impact
Businesses can focus on stocking and promoting top-performing products.

---

## Key Business Insights

### High-Value Customer Segment

✅ Married Women  
✅ Age Group 26–35 Years  
✅ Customers from Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka  
✅ Professionals from IT, Healthcare, and Aviation sectors
